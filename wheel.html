How does Jackpot work?

At the end of each month the admin draws a jackpot from all RGT that players lost on the wheel. Only net losses are counted.

Loss calculation

If you spent 5 RGT and won 3 RGT → loss = 2 RGT. If you won more than you spent → loss = 0.

Your chance formula

Your chance = Your loss / Total loss of all players. E.g. if you lost 5 RGT and total is 7 RGT → your chance = 5/7 ≈ 71%.
